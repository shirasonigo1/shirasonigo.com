Hey, I’m Shira.

A multidisciplinary engineer and designer who uses technology to solve real-world problems.

I started out as a software engineer in the Israel Defense Forces, where I led its cloud transformation. My team built the organisation’s first public cloud application, which kicked off a wider shift in how the IDF delivered software.

I then joined Forter as a cloud engineer, and went on to lead the company’s observability transformation and its adoption across engineering teams.

Ambition, curiosity and a love of adventure took me to London for the Innovation Design Engineering master’s at the Royal College of Art and Imperial College London, where I worked where technology, creativity and culture meet.

Today my practice brings together research, prototyping and systems thinking.

I’m looking for my next role. If you’re building something interesting, I’d love to grab a coffee.
