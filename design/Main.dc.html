CuLa

An accessible breast pump for blind and visually impaired mothers

Inclusive Design
Product Design
Co-Design Research
Prototyping

Co-designed with blind and visually impaired mothers, CuLa answers three problems no pump had solved for them: nipple alignment, awareness of milk flow, and operating the controller by touch alone. A magnetic flange, an acoustic bottle and a tactile controller, working together.
