<Chapter id="material" lead="Embracing waste as a resource for growth, using
simple methods with minimal transformation.">

Tire waste is a global issue that poses
significant risks to the environment, as
well as human and wildlife health. 
For years, discarded tires have been
repurposed for shoemaking, a practice
famously adopted by the Rarámuri (also
known as the Tarahumara), an Indige-
nous group from Mexico renowned for
their long-distance running in rubber
sandals.

<Insights>
  <Insight title="Pure natural rubber qualities">
    Inner tubes are strong, flexible, anti-slip and non-biodegradable.
  </Insight>
  <Insight title="The value is in the intact tube">
    Keeping the rubber whole preserves its integrity and avoids the toxic exposure that comes with re-processing it.
  </Insight>
</Insights>

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="process" lead="">

In this process it was important for me to use a construction method that preserves the natural integrity of the rubber no re-vulcanization, no cutting, no harmful glues. The process had to be sustainable as well. 
I took inspiration from traditional weaving craft. 
<MethodList>
  <Method title="Salvage">Used inner tubes collected from local bike shops, sorted and cleaned.</Method>
  <Method title="Cold construction">Formed the shoe without re-vulcanization, cutting or glue — keeping the rubber's structure intact and avoiding toxic particles.</Method>
  <Method title="Traditional craft">The shoe is hand made using a traditional shoe making practice and inpired by leather weaving</Method>
</MethodList>

<Chapter id="outcome" lead="Its durability and water-resistant qualities led me to explore its potential as an alternative material for water shoes">

By prototyping different weaving techniques and combining them with traditional handmade shoemaking methods, I developed a deep understanding of the material and its unique properties. Its durability and water-resistant qualities led me to explore its potential as an alternative material for water shoes, particularly those designed for walking and swimming on rocky beaches.
