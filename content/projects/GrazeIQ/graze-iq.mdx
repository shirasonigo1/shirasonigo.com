<Chapter id="research" lead="">

Our research included field interviews with farmers, veterinarians, and industry professionals. We immersed ourselves in the realities of day-to-day farming to better understand the challenges farmers face, from livestock management and pasture monitoring to financial and operational pressures.

<Insights>
  <Insight title="Farmers lack an accurate picture of their pasture">
   The most common methods for assessing grass availability rely heavily on visual judgement or manual tools which are not accurate.
  </Insight>
  <Insight title="Good grazing is a constant timing decision">
    Regenerative grazing depends on moving livestock at the right moment allowing the grass to be grazed without overgrazing and giving each paddock enough time to recover.
  </Insight>
  <Insight title="Existing tools create more work, rather than removing it">
    Current pasture-monitoring methods involve a trade-off between affordability, accuracy and effort. Simple tools such as sward sticks and plate meters require significant manual effort. 
    </Insight>
</Insights>

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="development" lead="We developed GrazeIQ through rapid prototyping and in-field testing">

We Moved between hardware, data collection and the farmer-facing experience. The opportunity is not simply to create another measurement tool, but to make pasture monitoring effortless and turn field data into clear, actionable guidance.
