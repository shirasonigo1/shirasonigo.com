<Chapter id="clockman" lead="The inspiration lies in the hands that shape our world.">

<PullQuote attribution="Menachem Sheinkin" context="Writings of Menachem Sheinkin, 1935">
  The craftsman takes on a known role in order to fulfil it completely. He invests all his talents in his work and devotes to it his entire soul... The work becomes art, free creation.
</PullQuote>

These hands belong to the artisans, the craftsmen, the ones whose trades tell stories of the past and bear witness to the evolution of our surroundings. In this work, I sought to immortalise the essence of a disappearing era. In the face of inevitable change, these hands — weathered by time and experience — carry a profound connection to the very fabric of the community I call home.

<Chapter id="legacy" lead="Carrying the memory of these artisans into the future.">

As the sands of time threaten to bury the crafts of yesteryear, "Clayful Hands edition" becomes a vessel, carrying the memories of these artisans into the future. Beyond the ticking of clocks, the work aspires to inspire the next generation — ensuring that the wisdom and spirit of these skilled hands live on. Even as the role of a clock-fixer fades into obscurity, the lessons of their timeless mastery echo in the hands of those who follow.
